## PROCESS: How We Work

A production system built for executive speed and keynote-grade quality.

**01 · Intake & Alignment** Every engagement starts from the all-up narrative and the public-sector messaging framework: audience, singular objective, and what success looks like in the room. The brief gets faster over time as we internalize the AWS voice and how each business team frames big ideas.

**02 · Narrative & Speaker Story** Slides are the last thing we think about. First we map the throughline: where the argument peaks, what has to land emotionally. Then we meet each speaker to shape their segment so it fits one cohesive arc, not five disconnected talks.

**03 · Scripting & Content** We turn frameworks and raw talking points into damn good copy in authentic AWS tone: scripts, talk tracks, and speaker notes that sound like the people delivering them.

**04 · Deck Modification & Build** Our designers adapt the core symposium deck per audience: bold where it needs to be, precise where it matters, always on-brand. We work natively in PowerPoint, so every file is editable and production-ready.

**05 · Dry Runs & Rapid Revisions** We run the dry runs, hold version control, and absorb the two-days-out, nineteen-changes reality without breaking the file (or a sweat). Same-day and next-day turnarounds. On-site when it counts.

**06 · Final Delivery & Debrief** Full QA pass (animation, fonts, aspect ratio, backup PDF, speaker notes), then a clean handoff to your production agencies (Sleek, Uptown, TK). After each event we debrief on what landed, and that becomes the baseline for the next build. Every keynote gets tighter because we're compounding.

## PLATFORM: Full Transparency, Built In. For Leads and Speakers Alike.

One place for the whole program, with a right-sized view for everyone in it.

Both your program leads and your speakers work *directly* with the SketchDeck team inside one platform: annotate on slides, leave comments in context, message the designer or writer, and pull the latest file anytime. No email version chaos. No "which deck is final?" No black-box agency experience.

What makes it work is **differentiated access**:

- **1P team leads** get the control tower: every symposium, every speaker's status, every version, plus real-time budget, spend, turnaround, and QC metrics. Full visibility into how the program is running, without having to ask.  
- **Speakers and other stakeholders** get a focused, frictionless view: just their slides, their notes, and their feedback thread. They comment, talk directly to the team, and grab the latest file, without wading through program-wide data or budget they don't need to see.

Same platform, streamlined two ways: for a busy executive who just wants their slides right, and for a program owner who needs to see everything at once.

**AI-Assisted Project Briefs:** structured intake that captures the right information upfront, so designers and writers hit the ground running.

**Collaborative Visual Feedback:** annotate directly on slides; mark rounds, track changes, resolve comments in context. Speakers and leads both comment where it counts, never in a lost email chain.

**Central Project & Asset Library:** every deck, version, and brand asset in one searchable, secure, compliant home. Everyone always has the latest file, scoped to what they should see.

**Real-Time Metrics & Visibility:** leads see project status, turnaround performance, revision cycles, and spend in real time. Full program transparency, on tap.
